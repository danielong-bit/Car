Quick Tip: Down Payment Impact

A higher down payment directly reduces your borrowed loan principal (P). Because auto financing calculates interest on the total borrowed amount:

  • Lower Total Interest: Every +10% down payment reduces the principal, cutting hundreds or thousands of Ringgit in total interest paid across your loan tenure.
  • Lower Monthly Installment: Smaller principal means reduced monthly commitments.
💡 Tip: Increasing your down payment from 10% to 20% can save thousands in total bank interest over a 7-year tenure!
