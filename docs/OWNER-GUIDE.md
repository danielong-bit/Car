# Danny Recon owner guide

Open `/studio` on a computer and sign in with the approved ChatGPT staff account. Customer contact email and staff login identity are separate. Public visitors cannot edit the site or read viewing requests.

## Daily workflow

1. **Add a car:** Vehicles → Add vehicle. Enter unique Stock ID, make, model, year and numeric RM price. Leave status Draft until reviewed. Keep the illustration label enabled for unverified examples.
2. **Upload photos:** Select the car → Upload media, or drag files into its upload area. JPG, PNG and WebP up to 10 MB; MP4 up to 100 MB. Originals are retained. Uploaded files remain private until used in published content.
3. **Replace the cover:** Replace keeps the selected photo’s framing metadata. Set cover moves a chosen photo to the front. The first visible non-video item becomes the public cover.
4. **Arrange the gallery:** Drag photos or use arrows. Choose exterior/interior/detail/documents/video categories and visibility. Hide private documents before publishing.
5. **Frame a photo:** Frame → Desktop or Mobile. Drag the focal point, adjust zoom and aspect ratio, or Reset. Vehicle detail uses the aspect ratio; fixed-size showcase and inventory cards use focal point/zoom. Originals are not cropped or overwritten.
6. **Change a price:** Edit Price (RM), Save draft, Preview, Publish. The homepage, detail page, comparisons, calculator and enquiries read the same published record.
7. **Sold / remove a car:** Sold keeps its detail page but removes it from active inventory and prevents viewing requests. Archived removes its public detail page. Save and Publish to apply.
8. **Edit homepage:** Homepage Editor changes headline, theme, gallery layout, featured priority and section order/visibility. Preview the saved draft before publishing.
9. **Delivery stories:** Choose Delivery Stories; upload/replace a photo, write a factual caption, link a vehicle if appropriate and record actual customer permission. Enable Published only after consent. These are past deliveries, not available stock. No customer quotes are invented.
10. **Manage requests:** Customer Enquiries shows private website requests. Requested is not confirmed. Contact the customer, agree a time, then change status. The site does not automatically send email or WhatsApp notifications.
11. **Preview / publish:** Save draft persists staff-only changes. Preview opens the exact customer page with a staff banner. Publish validates required photos, media links and customer consent, then updates public content. Reload on a conflicting edit from another session.
12. **Recover:** Undo/Redo covers recent edits in the current tab. Activity & Versions restores an earlier saved version into the draft; preview then publish it. Advanced Tools exports a content backup or loads a backup into the draft. R2 originals are separate from this JSON backup and remain stored.

## Other tools

- Media Library: search, reuse, copy stable URLs, create a watermark copy, archive unused uploads. A published file cannot be archived until its references are removed and republished.
- Interactive Tours: select a vehicle, click to add a hotspot and drag to position. Use its own photos and facts. Duplicate layout deliberately clears vehicle facts and media. Audi uses photo fades; automatic video and 360 transitions remain disabled.
- Advanced Tools: import CSV or XLSX, first sheet, up to 500 cars. Headers: `stockId,brand,model,year,price,mileage,engine,body`. Imported cars are drafts; review and add covers before publishing. Numeric RM price, no currency symbol. No macro or old `.xls` import.
- Financing Settings: enter an indicative effective annual rate and disclosure. Customer comparisons stay on their own device. The planner is not a bank approval; the lender’s final schedule controls.
- JPJ estimator: private petrol/diesel vehicles in Peninsular Malaysia, exact cc and AB/AC/AD/AE usage code. Other regions, EVs and special classes require a confirmed quote. Insurance and registration are entered quotes.

## Before real business use

Replace every illustration listing with documented real stock and exact vehicle photos. Confirm customer consent, descriptions, stock status, engine cc, legal registration class and lender terms. Keep the studio open only for your authorized account. Monitor the enquiry dashboard regularly because notifications are not configured.

## Vehicle introductions and social links
Vehicles > Danny’s introduction: enter this car’s TikTok video URL or the permanent uploaded MP4 URL from Media Library. Optional Facebook Reel fallback. An empty video URL hides the section. Layout duplication clears the presenter video to avoid using another car’s introduction. The supplied Audi S5 Avant links are assigned to Audi only. Brand & Profile has a Facebook page/profile field; while empty, the homepage Facebook card opens the supplied Audi Reel. Homepage social cards do not embed car videos.

Motion: circular theme reveal where View Transitions are supported; corner fill on contact/social cards; sliding navigation indicator; sticky contact portrait and vehicle gallery on desktop; expandable secondary photo/video and undo/redo controls. Reduced-motion preference disables animated transitions; touch devices show tool labels directly.

## Danny Recon Select homepage (v24)
The reference layout uses a navy hero and electric blue shared accent. Home, Inventory, Calculator, About Danny and Recent Deliveries are the five navigation links. Search opens the existing inventory filters; View All expands the four featured cards into all active listings. Mobile featured cards and delivery photos swipe horizontally, with a fixed WhatsApp action. About Danny contains contact information and social links. The hero background is an illustration and Danny’s cutout is an AI-assisted edit of the owner’s supplied photograph; it does not establish actual stock.
