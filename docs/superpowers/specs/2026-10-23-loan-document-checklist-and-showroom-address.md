# Loan Documents and Showroom Address

## Goal

Show customers the business address and a clear, multilingual checklist of documents commonly requested when they proceed with vehicle financing.

## Approved placement

- Show the supplied address in the contact/showroom block and use that same address for map links.
- Keep vehicle cards concise; use the address on vehicle details where customers arrange a viewing.
- Add a native expandable document checklist directly beneath the vehicle monthly payment calculator, before the customer finishes their enquiry.
- Add a shortcut from the vehicle enquiry section that opens the checklist.
- Make the list available in English, Chinese, and Malay.

## Content and privacy

List identification (IC/MyKad or accepted equivalent and driving licence), recent 3–6 months of payslips, recent 3–6 months of bank statements, EPF statement, and latest income tax filing/statement. State that bank requirements vary. Do not add an upload field or ask customers to send sensitive documents through public forms/comments; advise them to confirm a private submission method with Danny.

## Owner editing

Keep the address in the existing profile location setting and label it clearly as a full business/showroom address. Do not change owner cloud data, publish, or deploy as part of this local implementation.

## Verification

Build the local site and inspect the address/map links and the expandable checklist in the vehicle detail view. Check English, Chinese, and Malay labels. Do not submit an enquiry or transmit documents.
